# Q-Series Saturn Sequence Test

## Hypothesis tested

A broad sign-ingress/structural-consolidation hypothesis was tested:

> Major life-event dates may occur more often while transiting Saturn is in the person's natal Saturn sign, with a narrower exact-degree return window.

This is the Q-series analogue of the Anura case. It is a preliminary screen, not a final validation.

## Results

The available Q candidate-event file contains 161 usable date rows. These are not fully verified and are dominated by death candidates.

| Exposure | Candidate events | Matched control dates |
|---|---:|---:|
| Saturn in natal Saturn sign | 19/161 = 11.8% | 38/450 = 8.4% |
| Saturn within 3° of natal Saturn degree | 4/161 = 2.5% | 7/450 = 1.6% |
| Saturn retrograde | 60/161 = 37.3% | 171/450 = 38.0% |

Approximate descriptive ratios:

- Same natal Saturn sign: **1.40×** event/control exposure
- Within 3° exact return: **1.60×** event/control exposure
- Saturn retrograde: **0.98×** event/control exposure

These are not inferential results. The controls are preliminary random age-window dates and the event rows are candidate extractions.

## Interpretation

The screen gives a weak descriptive indication that Saturn-in-natal-sign exposure may be somewhat more common in the candidate event dates than in the preliminary controls. It does **not** reproduce a strong universal Saturn-return pattern.

The retrograde result is essentially identical to the control rate. This argues against treating retrograde status alone as the main signal in this sample.

The exact-degree return result is based on only four event rows, so it is too small to support a claim.

## Major limitations

- Candidate events have not been manually verified.
- Wealth, success, failure and poverty outcomes are not labelled.
- Death candidates dominate the sample.
- Event dates may be year/month approximations.
- Multiple rows can come from the same person.
- Controls are not yet matched on season, country, historical period or event opportunity.
- No causal inference is possible.

## Next test

To test the Anura-style sequence properly, add verified events and define three windows before analysis:

1. **Pre-ingress pressure window:** 180 days before Saturn enters natal Saturn sign.
2. **Sign-ingress consolidation window:** Saturn in natal Saturn sign but more than 3° from natal degree.
3. **Exact-return window:** Saturn within a pre-registered degree orb of natal Saturn.

Then classify outcomes as pressure, entry, consolidation, culmination or negative structural change and test them against person-level matched controls.
