# Bill Gates: Saturn Libra Leadership Case

## Date-only natal calculation

For 28 October 1955, Lahiri sidereal UTC-noon midpoint:

- Natal Saturn: **28°16′ Libra**
- Natal Saturn sign: Libra
- Midpoint motion: direct

## Historical timing correction

Bill Gates was president and chairman when Microsoft reorganized on 25 June 1981. Microsoft entered the operating-systems business with IBM PC/MS-DOS in 1981. A historical timeline also describes him as president from 1977–1982.

Saturn did **not** occupy Libra for all of 1982. In the date-only sidereal calculation:

- Saturn was in Virgo through most of 1982.
- Saturn entered Libra around **6 October 1982**.
- Saturn was in Libra only during the final part of 1982.

Therefore, the leadership/MS-DOS transition belongs mainly to a **pre-ingress pressure/transition phase**, not a full natal-sign Saturn phase. If a specific leadership milestone occurred after 6 October 1982, it can be classified as early natal-sign ingress exposure.

## Exact return

The estimated exact first Saturn return to approximately 28° Libra is **5 December 1984**, age about 29.1. This is separate from the 1981–82 leadership event.

## Research classification

This case supports the broader sequence hypothesis:

1. Leadership and operating-system expansion before Saturn's Libra ingress.
2. Saturn's Libra ingress in late 1982 as a structural phase transition.
3. Exact-degree return in late 1984 as a later maturity/culmination window.

It does not show that Saturn caused Microsoft's success. The historical record also includes product development, IBM's PC launch, organizational decisions and business execution.
