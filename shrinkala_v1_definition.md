# SHRINKALA v1.0 — Rule Definition
## Frozen for Testing on 25 Jul 2026

---

## 1. FORMATION RULE

A Shrinkala (Śrṅkhalā) is a **closed-loop chain of planetary disposition** involving 
**three or more planets** where:

- Planet A occupies the sign ruled by Planet B
- Planet B occupies the sign ruled by Planet C
- ...
- Planet N occupies the sign ruled by Planet A

This creates a loop where each planet's dispositor is the next planet in the chain,
and the final planet's dispositor is the first planet.

## 2. BOND STRENGTH (from Srinkhala document, scribd #673088430)

| Chain Length | Bond Strength | Classification |
|---|---|---|
| 2 planets (Parivartana) | 100 | Full exchange |
| 3 planets (Shrinkala) | 50 | Meaningful |
| 4 planets | 33 | Weakened |
| **5 planets** | **25** | **"Effect not considerable — IGNORE"** |

> Source: "It is best to consider only a 3-planet Srinkhala in a chart for delineating 
> purposes; and ignore the same if there are more planets involved as the effect of 
> Srinkhala is not considerable."

## 3. PLANET BEHAVIOR IN THE CHAIN

All planets in the Shrinkala chain "behave as if they are placed in their own signs."
This means:
- They gain own-sign strength (Swakshetra avastha)
- Their dignity is upgraded
- Natural debilitation/enemy-sign placement is mitigated

## 4. HOUSE CONNECTION

The houses occupied by the chain planets, AND the houses they rule, 
become linked through the disposition loop.

## 5. CLASSIFICATION

| Type | Houses Involved | Quality |
|---|---|---|
| **Maha Shrinkala** | Only auspicious houses (1,2,4,5,7,9,10,11) | Wealth, status, success |
| **Dainya/Khala** | Any dusthana lord (6,8,12) involved | Mixed — obstacles + breakthroughs |

## 6. DASHA ACTIVATION

The Shrinkala activates during the Mahadasha or Antardasha of any planet in the chain.
Effects intensify when multiple chain planets' dashas run consecutively.

## 7. D9 (NAVAMSA) RULE (Hypothesis Under Test)

HYPOTHESIS: If the Shrinkala is confirmed in D9 (i.e., the same planets form a chain 
or exchange in Navamsa), the yoga is strengthened. If D9 breaks the chain, the yoga 
is weakened.

## 8. 5-LOOP SPECIFIC RULE (Under Test)

Per the source document: "5-planet Shrinkala bond = 25. The effect is not considerable. 
Ignore if more than 3 planets are involved."

**TEST HYPOTHESIS:**
- H0 (null): 5-loop Shrinkala has NO predictive value beyond random expectation
- H1: 5-loop Shrinkala produces distinguishable patterns (even if weak)
- H1a: 5-loop + D9 Parivartana confirmation strengthens the yoga
- H1b: 5-loop in auspicious houses = positive outcomes
- H1c: 5-loop with dusthana lords = negative/mixed outcomes

## 9. MEASUREMENT CRITERIA

For each chart with 5-loop Shrinkala, measure:
1. Achievement score (0-10 scale from existing data)
2. Profession/outcome
3. D9 Parivartana presence
4. Dusthana lord involvement
5. Current Mahadasha lord (is it in the chain?)
