Method contract

What the assistant will and will not do

Will do

Extract source text, list planetary positions, calculate date-only research features supplied by the dataset, show uncertainty and compare event dates with pre-defined transit windows.

Will not do

Invent birth times, houses, marriage or wealth dates; turn a sign correlation into causation; or present a prediction as a fact.
