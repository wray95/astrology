# Understanding Lahiri Ayanamsa Accuracy
*Source: https://www.scribd.com/document/193546797/ayanamsa-1 (3 pages, uploaded by gift108)*
*Fetched 2026-08-02. Q&A from classical astrologers on ayanamsa choice.*

## Q: Is there any ayanamsa more accurate than others? How is ayanamsa determined?
**A (Gauranga Das):** The Indian Calendar Reform Committee concluded **Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) Ayanamsha is the most correct** available, though it can still be off by a few seconds of arc. Calculation of Lahiri ayanamsa:
1. Date of coincidence of the two zodiacs (per the Calendar Committee): **day of vernal equinox, 285 AD**.
2. Rate of precession: **50⅓ seconds of arc per solar year** (attributed to Varāhamihira's *Pañcasiddhāntikā*).
3. Point of coincidence = opposite point to the exact middle of **Chitra nakshatra** (star Spica) = 180° of the sidereal zodiac; the opposite point = beginning of Aries / Ashvini.

## Q: Why use Lahiri and not any other?
**A (Pt. Sanjay Rath):** Litmus test of any ayanamsa = accuracy of its working. Lahiri was adopted by the Govt. of India after the Lahiri Committee of astrologers debated it. Inherent drawback: annual progression of **50″** vs actual **~50.23″/year**, causing minor inconsistencies. We use it because it has not been bettered.

**A (Narasimha Rao):** The only true test is that divisional charts give good results consistently. Never got consistent results from lower-level divisional charts with Raman or other ayanamsas. Example: Sanjay's D-24 — with Raman, Jupiter in Taurus (Venusian); with Lahiri, Jupiter in Pisces, which better describes traditional learning. Lahiri is more accurate but has at least a few arc-minutes of error.

## Q: Which ayanamsa for actual desha period? (Chitrapaksha/Lahiri, Raman, Deva-Detta, Krishnamurti…)
**A (Pt. Sanjay Rath):** None are fully correct — **ayanamsa cannot be a linear function**. Average precession rate is ~**50.2425″/year**, yet all ayanamsas use only 50″, so error creeps in. The universally used one is the **Lahiri Committee ayanamsa (Govt. of India)**. Knowledge/math base must be stronger: concept of Chitra (Chitra Paksha), yoga tara, etc. Others tried Revati (Revati Paksha), Moola, Rohini (Rahu–Sikhi axis / elliptical orbit reasoning). More proposals may come in the next ~20 years. Until then: **use Lahiri ayanamsa**.
