## 5. STATISTICAL ANALYSIS

### Test 1: Indira Gandhi — Moon-Saturn Parivartana

- **H₀:** Events are distributed randomly across her adult dashas
- **Test:** Binomial test — probability of ≥5 events in any single 19-year dasha out of 6 events total, where that dasha covers 28.4% of timeline
- **Result:** P(≥5|n=6, p=0.284) = 6×0.284⁵×(1-0.284) + 0.284⁶ = 0.0033 + 0.0002 = **p = 0.0035**
- **After Bonferroni correction** (3 Parivartanas tested): p = 0.0105
- **Verdict:** ✅ **SIGNIFICANT — survives correction**

### Test 2: Oprah Winfrey — Saturn-Venus Parivartana

- **H₀:** Events are distributed randomly across her adult dashas
- **Test:** 1/4 events in Venus MD (25% observed vs 25% base rate)
- **p-value:** ~1.0 (cannot reject null)
- **Verdict:** ❌ **NO SIGNAL detected**

### Test 3: Moon-Jupiter Exchange (Oprah)
