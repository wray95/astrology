## 4. STATISTICAL TEST

**Test:** Is 5-loop achievement different from other groups?

Null hypothesis: 5-loop mean = overall mean (8.9)
Alternative: 5-loop mean < overall mean

Cannot test with n=3. Statistical significance requires n ≥ 30 per group
for reasonable power at medium effect size.

**Conclusion:** The direction (5-loop < others) is CONSISTENT with the 
traditional "ignore 5-loop" rule. But this is an OBSERVATION, not a validated finding.
