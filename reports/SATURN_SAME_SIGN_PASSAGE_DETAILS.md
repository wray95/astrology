# Saturn Same-Sign Passage Details

Generated for all 5,010 Q people and the separate 9 P people.

For every person, the output lists every contiguous Saturn sidereal passage through that person's natal Saturn sign from 1800 through 31 December 2028, plus:

- Saturn ingress date
- Saturn exit date
- 180-day pre-ingress window
- nearest date to the natal Saturn degree during the passage
- angular error from natal degree
- past/current/future status

## Interpretation rule

A pre-ingress window is defined as the 180 days before Saturn enters the natal Saturn sign. The sign passage is broader than an exact Saturn return. The nearest-degree date is calculated separately and should be used for exact-return analysis.

This output provides the full timing windows for event matching; it does not claim that a person will experience a particular event during any window.

No birth times, Ascendants, houses, D1 or D9 charts were used. Future windows are astronomical research windows, not predictions.
