## 📜 GULIKA FUNDAMENTALS

### What Is Gulika?
- **Gulika = Maandi** (synonymous per BPHS: "Of the same, another name is Maandi")
- No physical body — a **sub-planet** (upagraha) like Rahu/Ketu
- Represents **Saturn's segment of time** (the most malefic entity in a chart)
- Per Dr. Suresh Chandra Mishra: "Surpasses all natural malefics in maleficence"
- Gulika-Kundali used to be an essential component of traditional Janma-patri

### Calculation Method
1. Divide day (sunrise→sunset) or night (sunset→sunrise) into **8 equal parts**
2. **Daytime**: 1st segment = day's weekday lord, then in natural order
3. **Nighttime**: 1st segment = 5th weekday from the day lord, then in natural order
4. The **8th segment is lordless** in both cases
5. **Saturn's segment = Gulika**. Rising sign at START of Gulika's segment = Gulika position.

> ⚠️ Prashna Marga says the ghati method is "not accurate." The 8-part division method is authoritative (BPHS).

## ⏳ GULIKA DASHA RULES

1. **Dasha of Gulika's dispositor or its navamsha lord = HAZARDOUS** (Smita Patil example: died in Saturn-Mars; Saturn = Gulika's rashi lord, Mars = navamsha lord)
2. **Gulika in 1, 5, or 9** → Lagna lord becomes maraka during its dasha
3. **Dasha of any planet associating with Gulika** in rashi, navamsha, or dwadashamsha = trouble
4. **Pramaana Gulika** (180° opposite) = equally malefic, same treatment
5. Transits of Saturn/Jupiter over Gulika's navamsha lord, Sun over dwadashamsha lord, Moon over trimshamsha lord = trouble
