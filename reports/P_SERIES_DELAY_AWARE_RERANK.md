## Why the order is retained

The Q sample shows only weak descriptive evidence for Saturn pre-ingress and natal-sign phases, and no verified Q wealth/success trajectories. Applying those rates to P would create false precision. The current P order remains a prior exploratory ranking until P event dates and Q outcome labels are verified.

## Required P data for a true rerank

For each P person, collect dated attempts, career entry, promotion, business founding, wealth gain, marriage, childbirth, failure, bankruptcy and recovery events. Then classify each event relative to Saturn pre-ingress, natal-sign, exact-return and post-sign phases.
