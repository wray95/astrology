# Elon Musk: Saturn Taurus Pre-Ingress Wealth Case

## Date-only natal calculation

For 28 June 1971, using the Lahiri sidereal UTC-noon midpoint:

- Natal Saturn: **7°41′ Taurus**
- Saturn sign: Taurus
- Natal Saturn midpoint motion: direct

## Wealth event and Saturn timing

The sale of Zip2 to Compaq in February 1999 made Musk a multi-millionaire; reports commonly cite a personal proceeds figure of approximately US$22 million. The transaction is documented in the Zip2 reference material and contemporary reporting.

On the sale date, date-only Saturn was approximately:

- **5° Aries**, direct
- Saturn was not yet in Taurus

Saturn entered sidereal Taurus on approximately **7 June 2000**, about 16 months after the wealth event. It therefore fits a possible **pre-ingress / lead-in event** pattern rather than an exact Saturn-return pattern.

The exact-degree Saturn return to natal 7° Taurus is a separate, narrower event and should not be equated with the 1999 wealth milestone.

## Research interpretation

This case supports a testable sequence hypothesis:

> A major wealth transition may occur before Saturn enters the native's natal Saturn sign, with the later sign passage representing consolidation, scaling or structural consequences.

It does not establish that Saturn caused the Zip2 sale. The event could be explained by the company's acquisition, internet-market conditions, entrepreneurship and business execution. The correct Q-series test is whether the same pre-ingress pattern appears more often than matched controls across many verified wealth events.
