## 🔬 RONNIE GALE DREYER'S PREDICTION METHOD (Step-by-Step)

Dreyer's article provides the most accessible **step-by-step prediction framework**:

### STEP 1: Analyze the Lagna (Ascendant)
- Sign qualities (cardinal/fixed/mutable, element)
- House cusp (whole-sign houses)
- Planets in the 1st house

### STEP 2: Analyze the Lagna Lord (Chart Ruler)
- Sign + house placement = dominating life area
- Nakshatra of Lagna lord = deeper life purpose
- Dignity: own/exalted/debilitated/neutral
- **"If the ruling planet is beneficial, the person should fulfill destiny without too many obstacles"**

### STEP 3: Planetary Associations (Drishti / Aspects)
- Natural benefics = strengthen. Natural malefics = weaken.
- Judged by PLANETS involved, NOT by aspect type (square, trine, etc.)
- Conjunctions (yogas) = powerful associations
- Special aspects: Mars(4,7,8), Jupiter(5,7,9), Saturn(3,7,10)

### STEP 4: Check All Planets' Dignity
- Own sign (Swakshetra) = strongest
- Exaltation (Uchcha) = very strong
- Debilitation (Neecha) = problematic for that planet's karaka
- Neecha Bhanga = cancellation of debilitation

### STEP 5: Apply Dasha Timing
- Vimshottari: Ketu(7)→Venus(20)→Sun(6)→Moon(10)→Mars(7)→Rahu(18)→Jupiter(16)→Saturn(19)→Mercury(17)
- **"The qualities of dasas are determined by the general quality of the planetary ruler, the sign and house the planet occupies and rules, and the planetary associations."**
