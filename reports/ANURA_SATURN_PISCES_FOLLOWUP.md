## Corrected timeline

Anura Kumara Dissanayake won the presidential election on **21 September 2024** and was sworn in on 23 September. The NPP then won the parliamentary election on **14 November 2024**, securing a supermajority; this was not a 2025 parliamentary election. In 2025, the relevant later electoral milestone was the **6 May 2025 local-government election**, where the NPP won a decisive majority of local councils but with a reduced national vote share.

## The evidence-based interpretation

The proposed pattern can be stated as a testable hypothesis:

> For people with natal Saturn in Pisces, major political or career consolidation may be more frequent during Saturn's later Pisces sign passage, including the exact-degree return window.

The historical sequence is consistent with a **broad sign-ingress hypothesis** because the NPP's May 2025 local-election victory occurred while Saturn was in Pisces. It is not an exact Saturn-return event: the exact-degree second return is estimated for April 2027.

The 2024 presidential and parliamentary victories also occurred before Saturn entered Pisces, while Saturn was in Aquarius. Therefore the data does not support the stronger claim that Saturn in Pisces was necessary for Anura's initial rise to power. It may be more appropriate to distinguish:

- 2024: presidential and parliamentary consolidation while Saturn was in Aquarius
- 2025: local-government consolidation during Saturn's Pisces sign ingress
- 2027: exact-degree second Saturn return, a future test window in this date-only model

## Conclusion

This is a useful case study for a two-level Gochara test—sign ingress versus exact-degree return—but it does not establish causation. The strongest confirmatory design would compare many leaders with natal Saturn in Pisces against leaders with natal Saturn in other signs, using election wins, losses, coalition changes and government crises as pre-defined outcomes.
