## 4. KEY FINDINGS

### Finding 1: 5-Loop Mean Achievement is Lowest
Mean achievement for 5-loop (7.0) is substantially lower than all other groups (8.9–9.3). However, **n=3** is insufficient for statistical significance.

### Finding 2: 5-Loop Shows Lowest Vargottama
Mean vargottama count for 5-loop (0.7) is lower than all other groups (0.9–1.4). This suggests the D1 chain does NOT carry through to D9 confirmation.

### Finding 3: Bond Strength Hierarchy is Consistent
Mean achievement follows bond strength: 50 > 100 > 33 > 0 > 25. The 3-loop (bond=50) has the highest mean achievement (9.26), consistent with the traditional document which says 3-loop Srinkhala is the meaningful threshold.

### Finding 4: Shakira is an Outlier
Shakira (ach=9) has 5-loop but is highly successful. Stan Lee (ach=8) is moderately successful. Senith (ach=4) is the lowest in the entire dataset. Without Shakira, the 5-loop mean would be 6.0.
