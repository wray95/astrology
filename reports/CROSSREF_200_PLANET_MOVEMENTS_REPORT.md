# 200-Person Planetary Cross-Reference

The 200-person baseline was cross-referenced against date-only Lahiri sidereal positions for Saturn, Mars, Venus, Jupiter, Rahu and Ketu.

## Coverage

- Birth-position records: 200/200
- Event rows with usable dates: 0

The baseline JSON contains mostly living people and did not include structured career, marriage, wealth, success or death dates. Therefore the event-transit table is empty rather than fabricated.

Rahu and Ketu are modeled as mean lunar nodes, which conventionally move retrograde; their flags are not comparable to a planet's occasional retrograde periods.

Most frequent birthday signs in this 200-person selection:

- Saturn: Sagittarius 23, Scorpio 22, Aries 20
- Mars: Sagittarius 29, Virgo 26, Libra 18
- Venus: Scorpio 42, Sagittarius 29, Capricorn 28
- Jupiter: Sagittarius 22, Virgo 22, Capricorn 21
- Rahu: Libra 21, Capricorn 20, Scorpio 19
- Ketu: Aries 21, Cancer 20, Taurus 19

These are descriptive birth-date distributions, not event correlations. To cross-reference planetary movements with success, wealth, marriage or failure, exact dated events must be added first.
