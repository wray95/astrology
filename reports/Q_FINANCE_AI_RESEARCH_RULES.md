# Q-Series Financial AI Rules

## What the AI may use without birth time

- Planetary sign and degree positions
- Moon-anchored Gochara when Moon sign is stable across the date
- Sun-sign and planetary-sign sensitivity analyses
- Jupiter, Saturn, Venus, Mars and Rahu/Ketu sign changes
- Returns, ingress, retrograde and station proxies
- Transit-to-natal-degree contacts
- Three-year lead/core/lag windows
- Verified financial event dates

## What the AI must not claim without birth time

- 2nd, 6th, 10th or 11th houses
- House lords or a financial trine
- Lagna-based Gochara
- D9 or D10 results
- Vimshottari Dasha/Antardasha timing
- House-based Dhana Yogas
- Prashna Kundali results

## Financial outcome definitions

Use objective labels:

- first paid work
- business founded
- first documented million or equivalent threshold
- company sale or IPO
- peak documented net worth
- bankruptcy or major financial loss
- debt crisis
- inheritance
- financial recovery

Do not infer “rich” or “poor” from fame, occupation or a planetary position. Unknown must remain unknown.

## Discovery protocol

1. Discover features on Q people only.
2. Use positive, negative and null cases.
3. Compare six-month, one-year, two-year and three-year windows.
4. Match controls by person, age, month/season, geography and historical period.
5. Cluster uncertainty by person.
6. Correct for multiple feature searches.
7. Lock the candidate rules before evaluating P-series predictions.

Nadi, earthy-sign concentration and Prashna may be included as explicitly labelled exploratory hypotheses, but they must not be presented as validated financial predictors.
