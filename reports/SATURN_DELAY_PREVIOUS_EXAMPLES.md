# Saturn Delay Review: Previous Case Studies

## Research rule

“Saturn delays” must be operationalized before testing. Here, delay means a measurable interval between an initial attempt/role and a later outcome milestone, compared with a defined benchmark or control group. Saturn cannot be assigned a delay merely because an event happened after a Saturn transit.

## Previous examples

| Person | Natal Saturn | Initial event | Later milestone | Observed interval |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Jeff Bezos | ~28° Capricorn | Amazon founded July 1994 | First annual profit in 2003 | ~9 years |
| Bill Gates | ~28° Libra | Microsoft leadership/MS-DOS period 1981 | Windows 1.0 retail release 1985 | ~4 years from Libra ingress; ~2 years after 1983 announcement |
| Elon Musk | ~8° Taurus | Zip2 founded 1995 | Zip2 sale/multi-millionaire 1999 | ~4 years; before Taurus ingress in 2000 |
| Anura Dissanayake | ~26° Pisces | Presidential victory Sep 2024 | Local-government consolidation May 2025 | ~7.5 months; before exact return, during Pisces ingress |

## What appears compatible with a delay hypothesis

- **Bezos:** Amazon's founding preceded profitability by many years. This is the clearest operational delay in the examples, but it is also normal for a technology company and cannot be attributed to Saturn without controls.
- **Bill Gates:** Microsoft leadership and MS-DOS expansion preceded the later Windows retail product by several years. This is compatible with a structure-first, visible-result-later sequence.
- **Elon Musk:** The first wealth event was not delayed until Saturn entered Taurus; it occurred before the ingress. Later SpaceX/Tesla outcomes took years to mature, so the case may support delayed consolidation rather than delayed initial success.
- **Anura:** National power came first, followed by broader local-government consolidation after Saturn entered Pisces. The exact-degree Saturn return is still a future window, so no exact-return outcome can yet be claimed.

## What is not established

The examples do not show that everything Saturn touches is delayed. They show several ordinary business/political sequences with different durations. A delay may result from product development, financing, regulation, competition, elections, market conditions or organizational complexity.

## Q-series test needed

For each Q person, collect:

- attempt/start date
- intermediate milestone date
- success/wealth date
- failure or abandonment date
- event type and source confidence

Then compare delay durations for people exposed to:

1. Saturn pre-ingress
2. Saturn natal-sign phase
3. Saturn exact-degree return
4. Saturn retrograde/station
5. Matched non-Saturn control periods

Use survival analysis or time-to-event models, censoring incomplete careers and clustering repeated events by person. Only then can we estimate whether Saturn phases are associated with longer time-to-success.
