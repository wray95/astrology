- Natal Saturn: Taurus 7.69° (UTC-noon baseline)

2. THE THEORETICAL DILEMMA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This creates two competing interpretations for the "Saturn Engine":
a) False Positive / Random Timing: The wealth event is decoupled from Saturn's 
   position, and the subsequent PayPal events during the return are coincidental.
b) The Pre-Ingress Trigger Hypothesis: Major structural wealth crystallization 
   frequently occurs during the final decan of the preceding sign (Aries 20°-30°), 
   acting as a pressure-build phase before the actual sign ingress and return.

3. METHODOLOGICAL CONSTRAINTS & DATA QUALITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Birth Data Confidence: Rodden Rating C (date-only UTC-noon estimate). House placements 
  and exact minute houses are unverified, though planetary sign degrees remain 
  within a 1° tolerance over the course of the birth date.
- Verification Standard: Strict external documentation required for financial 
  liquidity events (M&A exits, IPOs, major asset liquidations).
