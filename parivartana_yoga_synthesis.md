### Visti Larsen (SJC Europe 2005):

**Three levels of analysis:**
- **Rasi Parivartana** — Sign exchange (D1, D9, D60)
- **Jiva Parivartana** — Nakshatra lord exchange
- **Karaka Parivartana** — House karaka exchange (e.g., Sun in 5th + Jupiter in 1st)

**Rule 1:** The planet associated with Lagna/Lagnesh/Arudha Lagna = **focus of the change**
**Rule 2:** The Chara Karaka of planets involved = **people involved in transformation**
**Guna analysis:** Sattva↔Rajas↔Tamas direction determines auspiciousness

## 🎯 THE TRADITIONAL TRUTH (7 Sources Converge)

> 1. **Not all exchanges are equal.** Maha > Kahala > Dainya. Flat "bond = 100" scoring misses this entirely.
>
> 2. **Dainya means "poverty/misery."** A dusthana-tainted exchange (like P1's and P2's) damages the auspicious planet involved. Real outcomes confirm: P1 has wealth but through difficulty; P2 has foreign success but moderate domestic wealth.
>
> 3. **Kendra-Kona exchange = Raj Yoga.** Only Senath's Venus(9th Kona)-Jupiter(4th/7th Kendra) qualifies pure. P2's Jupiter(9th Kona)-Saturn(10th Kendra) is structurally Kendra-Kona but tainted by 12th lordship.
>
> 4. **Dignity > Exchange.** P4 proves you don't need Parivartana at all when planets are dignified (Malavya + Dhana + Lakshmi).
>
> 5. **Nakshatra Parivartana (Jiva) matters too.** The Cancer-Leo "single entity" principle opens a deeper layer not yet explored in the six charts.
>
> 6. **BPHS 11-12 is clear:** "If exchange between Kendra lord and Kona lord → Raj Yoga → become a king, be famous." Only Senath qualifies this purely.
