# P-Series Rerank After Q-Series Rerun

## Decision

The Q rerun does **not justify a transit-based change** to the P-series ranking.

The Q signal is weak and exploratory:

- Pre-ingress: 5.6% of candidate events vs 2.8% controls
- Natal Saturn sign: 9.3% vs 7.0%
- Exact return within 3°: 2.5% vs 1.4%
- Saturn retrograde: not elevated versus controls

The event sample lacks verified Q wealth, success, failure, marriage and childbirth outcomes. Applying these weak descriptive rates to P charts would create false precision.

## Why no change

A valid P prediction model requires verified Q outcomes to estimate whether pre-ingress, natal-sign or exact-return exposure predicts a defined result. The current Q rerun has candidate dates but no reliable outcome labels. P has no complete verified event timeline either.

The P charts have been recalculated with date-only planetary positions, but this identifies research windows rather than ranking outcomes.
