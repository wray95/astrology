# Advanced No-Birth-Time Methods: Research Policy

## Rashi/sign placements

Use sign and degree placements as date-only features when stable. Do not turn them into houses or house-lord claims.

## Moon-anchored Gochara

Use the natal Moon sign as a reference only when stable across the civil birth date. Moon nakshatra and sign should carry a sensitivity flag when the Moon moves during the date.

## Nadi-style degree interactions

Nadi-style sign/degree conjunction and planetary-chain rules can be included as explicitly labelled hypothesis features. They must be compared with controls and cannot be called accurate merely because they produce a plausible story.

## Birth-time rectification

BTR can be implemented as a candidate-ranking module when a person has:

- a bounded birth-time interval
- dated life events collected independently of the chart theory
- a pre-registered scoring rule
- a held-out event set

BTR becomes circular if the time is selected because it produces the desired wealth or career explanation. It should output candidate times and uncertainty, not a certified correct time.

## Prashna

Prashna requires the exact time and location at which a question was asked. It cannot be retroactively used to replace a missing natal time in the Q database. It is a separate prospective study design for new questions.

## Financial research

Without birth time, do not use 2nd/6th/10th/11th houses, house lords, D9, D10, Dasha or house yogas. Test sign/degree transits, Moon-anchored Gochara, Jupiter/Saturn/Venus/Rahu/Ketu cycles and verified financial event dates instead.
